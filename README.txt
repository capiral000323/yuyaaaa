# For Yuya ♡

A cute / elegant / dark-romantic / kawaii love website made by Caps.

## Files

- `index.html` — main website
- `style.css` — design and animations
- `script.js` — relationship counter, music, photo lightbox, effects
- `photos/photo1.jpg` through `photo7.jpg` — replace these with your 7 photos
- `d14u-hellmery.mp3` — put your legally obtained/local copy of the song here

## How to use

1. Put your seven photos inside the `photos` folder.
2. Rename them exactly:
   - photo1.jpg
   - photo2.jpg
   - photo3.jpg
   - photo4.jpg
   - photo5.jpg
   - photo6.jpg
   - photo7.jpg
3. Put your music file in the main folder and name it `d14u-hellmery.mp3`.
4. Open `index.html` in a browser.

The relationship counter starts at February 10, 2026.
